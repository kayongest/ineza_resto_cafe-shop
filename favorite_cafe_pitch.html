INEZA RESTO & COFFEE SHOP App

Investment Proposal & Value Proposition

This document outlines why building a Progressive Web App (PWA) platform is the ultimate investment for INEZA RESTO & COFFEE SHOP, and why the 1,000,000 RWF pricing is an incredible value for the Return on Investment (ROI) it will generate.

5 Reasons to Invest in this PWA

1. The "Native App" Experience with Zero App Store Friction

Customers don't want to go to the Google Play Store or Apple App Store to download a heavy app just to order coffee or food. A PWA bypasses this completely. Customers simply visit your website link, and they can instantly "Install" the app directly to their phone's home screen. It looks, feels, and acts exactly like a native app—complete with a home screen icon, smooth animations, and offline capabilities—but takes up a fraction of the storage space.

2. Elimination of High Third-Party Commission Fees

Relying heavily on third-party delivery apps eats directly into the cafe's profit margins through aggressive commission fees (often 15% - 30% per order). By owning this independent ordering platform, INEZA RESTO & COFFEE SHOP takes 100% of its revenue. A one-time investment of 1,000,000 RWF pays for itself incredibly fast when you are no longer surrendering a massive cut of every digital order to a middleman.

3. Direct Customer Retention & Push Notifications

This platform turns one-time walk-ins into loyal, repeat customers. With features like the "Favorites" tab and the "Notifications" screen, the cafe has a direct line to the customer's pocket. The owner can send localized, targeted promotions directly to their device, driving immediate impulse sales during slow hours.

4. Cross-Platform Efficiency (3 Apps for the Price of 1)

To build a custom iOS app, a custom Android app, and a Desktop website traditionally costs millions of Rwandan Francs and requires three separate development teams. Because this is a PWA, the 1,000,000 RWF investment buys a unified system. The exact same codebase flawlessly powers the expansive desktop view on a laptop and the sleek, touch-friendly mobile app view on a smartphone.

5. Streamlined Operations & Reduced Wait Times

For an existing cafe, operational bottlenecks hurt the customer experience. This PWA allows customers to browse the dynamic visual menu, customize their meal, and place orders ahead of time. This frees up the cafe staff to focus on food quality and in-person hospitality rather than manually taking orders.

3 Major Promotional Capabilities

1. High-Visibility In-App Banners & Dynamic Carousels

The app features prime "digital real estate" right on the home screen. The large promotional banner immediately captures attention the second a customer opens the app. The owner can dynamically update these sliding banners to push daily specials, seasonal menus, or high-margin items.

2. Native Push Notifications for Instant Reach

Unlike emails or social media posts, a PWA allows the cafe to send Push Notifications directly to the lock screen of the customer's phone. If it's a slow Tuesday afternoon, the owner can instantly blast a targeted notification: "Flash Sale! Get a free pastry with any large coffee in the next 2 hours."

3. Integrated Promo Codes & The "Favorites" Loop

The platform is designed with a dedicated "Promocodes & Gift Cards" section, allowing the cafe to easily distribute discount codes to incentivize first-time app users. Additionally, the "Favorites" tab removes friction for repeat purchases. The easier it is for a customer to reorder their "usual", the more frequently they will buy it.

The Bottom Line: For 1,000,000 RWF, INEZA RESTO & COFFEE SHOP isn't just buying a website; they are acquiring a fully branded, digital storefront, an automated marketing channel, and an operational efficiency tool that will aggressively drive revenue and protect their profit margins.
