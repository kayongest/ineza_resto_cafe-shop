---

## 1. Core Value Propositions & Financial Pitch

When drafting proposals or pitch slides, emphasize the following metrics:

### A. The 0% Commission Advantage
- Third-party delivery aggregators charge **15% to 30%** per order.
- **Financial Calculation Formula**:
  - Example: A cafe processing 100 orders/day at 6,000 RWF average ticket size generates 18,000,000 RWF/month.
  - Aggregator commission (20% average) = **3,600,000 RWF/month lost**.
  - INEZA Resto platform eliminates this middleman fee completely.

### B. Table Turnover & Labor Efficiency
- QR code table ordering cuts waiter dispatch delays by **60%**.
- Customers order immediately upon seating without waiting for busy staff.
- Kitchen receives accurate, digital tickets with zero handwriting misinterpretations.

### C. Zero-Friction PWA (No App Store Download)
- Native mobile apps experience an 80%+ drop-off rate because users refuse to download 100MB apps for a single visit.
- This platform runs instantly in Safari, Chrome, Samsung Internet via QR scan or URL bookmark.

## 3. Recommended 4-Slide Pitch Structure

When asked to generate pitch slides:

- **Slide 1: The Executive Problem**: The high cost of delivery commissions, table wait bottlenecks, and disjointed restaurant operations in Rwanda.
- **Slide 2: The Solution (INEZA Ecosystem)**: 3-in-1 platform uniting Table QR Ordering, Real-Time Kitchen Display (KDS), and Direct Takeaway/Delivery.
- **Slide 3: Customer & Kitchen Experience**: Instant MoMo USSD prompt, live 7-stage order tracking, and 80mm thermal EBM receipt printing.
- **Slide 4: Financial ROI & Packages**: Show the monthly savings breakdown and custom onboarding quotation.
