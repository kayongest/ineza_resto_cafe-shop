Customer Action
Registration & Login

The customer opens the app. New users register via a quick form (Name, Phone, Email). Returning users log in to instantly see their past history and saved addresses, ensuring a frictionless experience.

Customer Action
Menu & Ordering

Browsing our dynamic database-driven menu, the customer discovers crave-worthy meals, adds items to their cart, specifies quantities, and chooses between Delivery, Takeaway, or Dine-in.

Customer Action
Payment & Receipt

The customer selects Cash, Card, or MoMo. An Order ID (e.g., INC-3825) is generated. The system securely saves the order to the MySQL database and provides a sleek digital invoice.

Admin Action
Kitchen Notified

The Admin Dashboard silently detects the new order in the database. A flashing notification appears on the Chef's screen, and the ticket is pinned to the Live Dispatch Board.

Admin Action
Status Advancements

As the Chef begins cooking, they update the ticket dropdown from "Kitchen Preparing" to "Ready for Delivery". This instantly writes back to the database, synchronizing the entire system.

Customer Action
Live Tracking

Meanwhile, the customer is viewing their Order Timeline on their phone. Because the Chef updated the DB, the customer's phone instantly turns the "Ready" step green via background polling!

Completion
Order Complete

The food is handed over to the driver or customer. Admin marks the ticket "Completed". The ticket is archived, analytics are updated, and the customer enjoys their meal!
